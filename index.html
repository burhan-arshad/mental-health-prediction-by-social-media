Privacy & responsible use

  • This tool provides a machine-learning estimate, not a medical diagnosis.
  • The result should not replace professional mental-health advice.
  • MindScope does not detect, diagnose or treat any mental-health condition.
  • Your answers are sent only to the prediction service this app is connected to, solely to compute your result. This page does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not send your data anywhere else.
